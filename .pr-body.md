## refactor(delivery): collapse 4 instant channel pipelines into `runInstantAttemptPipeline`

`deliverInstantEmailBatch`, `deliverInstantWhatsAppBatch`, `deliverInstantSlackBatch`,
and `deliverInstantTeamsBatch` were line-for-line identical for their first ~80 lines
(dedupe -> quiet-hours skip -> claim) and again across the finalize tail, differing only
in the channel string, provider constants, payload-snapshot extras, preparation, and the
actual send call.

This PR extracts that shared shape into one generic
`runInstantAttemptPipeline(env, config, input, sendFn)` (issue #2336), where `sendFn` is
the only per-channel code. Each channel becomes a thin wrapper supplying its config and
`sendFn`. Per-channel behavior is preserved exactly: channel strings, providers, payload
snapshots (subject/shortChange/watchlistUrl extras), quiet-hours handling, owned-claim
semantics (Window enforce on email/Slack/Teams, not WhatsApp), and the claim-protocol
marker are all retained per the original copy-and-paste bodies.

### Scope
- `files:` `app/lib/delivery.server.ts` only.
- Converted all four instant pipelines per the binding judge edits ("convert email first,
  `npm test` green, then convert Slack, WhatsApp, and Teams the same way").
- The four digest-target twins and the eight target resolvers are **out of scope** per the
  binding judge edits and are untouched.

### Line-count note (acceptance vs binding scope)
The issue's `accept:` line targets `delivery.server.ts` dropping under ~3,000 lines. That
number was computed assuming the full ~1,600-line matrix (instant pipelines **plus** the
four digest-target twins and eight target resolvers) is collapsed. The binding judge edits
explicitly exclude the digest twins and target resolvers from scope. Within the in-scope
instant-pipeline work only, the file drops from **4,365 to 4,014 lines** (-351). Reaching
under ~3,000 requires the out-of-scope digest-target collapse, which is not permitted
here.
