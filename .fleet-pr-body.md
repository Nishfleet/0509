## What & why

The `/ads/:domain` proof page showed two live ad counts from two different
fields with no distinguishing label: the H1 ownership claim (`brandOwnedAdCount`,
e.g. "2 of these 6") and the stat-line active count (`teaser.activeCount`,
e.g. "4 active at last check"). A visitor read one page saying 2 and 4
simultaneously — a self-contradiction a proof-first product cannot afford on
its public proof page (issue #2319).

This change labels both numbers so they cannot collide. The stat line now
reads `{activeCount} ads active at last check ({brandOwnedCount} brand-owned)`
(stale) / `{activeCount} ads active ({brandOwnedCount} brand-owned)` (fresh), so
"active" no longer competes with "brand-owned". Both values are real loader
fields passed into `BrandStatLine` as a prop (the one allowed route change) —
never hardcoded, and pluralized so a one-ad capture reads "1 ad". Per the judge
edits, "verified-linked" is dropped (no evidence `activeCount` counts only
verified-linked ads).
