## 5. Listing copy

### Tagline

Use this one line verbatim:

> Five to Nine watches competitors' Meta ads and landing pages, then sends
> screenshot evidence and change alerts before your next meeting.

(This mirrors the live homepage claim at `https://0509.io` — it is already
public customer-facing copy, so it stays honest in the directory.)

### Description

Paste-ready (~150 words; edit freely but keep the honesty constraints in
section 7):

> Five to Nine is proof-backed competitor monitoring for growth teams. It
> watches the competitors you already track — their Meta ads and their
> landing pages — and tells you when something actually changed, with
> screenshot evidence and a source trail you can open yourself.
>
> You can paste a competitor's site and preview their current ads before
> creating an account; coverage and freshness are labeled. After signup,
> Five to Nine monitors on a schedule (every 3–6 hours depending on plan),
> detects real changes, and delivers daily and weekly digests by email.
> Saved change records carry visible proof, and workspaces grow a
> compounding memory: collections, notes, tags, shareable links, reports,
> CSV/API exports, and a customer API + MCP endpoint for audited agent
> actions.
>
> Presence Desk extends the same proof-backed model to tracking your brand
> and competitors across declared sources (website/open-web active today).
> Plans: Free (1 competitor, weekly brief), Scout, Starter, and Agency, with
> metered monthly evidence checks and overflow packs — no unlimited
> monitoring claims. Checkout pricing is localized at purchase time.

### Features & specs

SaaSHub product pages show a "Features & Specs" list. Suggested items (each
1–2 sentences, matching shipped behavior):

1. **Proof-backed change alerts** — Every alert is tied to a saved evidence
   capture with a source trail, so a "change" is something you can open and
   verify, not a guess.
2. **Competitor Meta ads monitoring** — Watch the competitors you track and
   see new ads, changed offers, and creative shifts with a public search
   preview that works without an account.
3. **Landing-page change monitoring** — Landing pages are checked on a
   schedule; changed pages are captured as evidence with before/now
   comparison.
4. **Daily and weekly email digests** — Summaries of what changed, with
   priority, next action, and proof status, delivered on your plan's
   cadence (weekly on Free/Scout, daily + weekly on Starter/Agency).
5. **Presence Desk** — Track your brand and competitors across declared
   sources with proof-backed entity briefs (website/open-web source active;
   other sources gated until provider approval).
6. **Workspace memory** — Collections, notes, tags, manual external
   evidence, share links, reports, and CSV/API JSON exports that compound
   into reusable competitive intelligence.
7. **Customer API and MCP** — Account-owned readiness, exports, and narrow
   audited agent actions through `/api/v1` and `/api/mcp` with a customer
   API key.
8. **Localized checkout pricing** — Scout, Starter, and Agency plans with
   monthly and annual checkout previews localized for the buyer's location.
