# Five to Nine North Star

## Naming Decision

- Keep `0509` as the internal repo, infra, and domain handle until an explicit operational rename happens.
- Use `Five to Nine` as the customer-facing product name.

## Why This Name Works

- `0509` means nothing to customers on its own.
- `Five to Nine` turns the code name into a story customers can remember.
- The name points at the real problem: teams stop checking, competitors keep moving, and the next decision gets made with stale information.

## North-Star Story

`Five to Nine` closes the gap between when your team stops checking and when the next decision gets made.

Between end-of-day and the next standup, competitors launch new ads, change offers, swap landing pages, and shift the story. Most teams find out too late. `Five to Nine` watches that gap, confirms the real changes, and sends proof-backed alerts your team can trust.

## Core Promise

- `See what changed, with proof.`

## One-Liner

- `Five to Nine tells growth teams what competitors changed on Meta, shows the proof, and sends the right alerts before the next workday starts.`

## Short Pitch

- `Most teams do competitor research, but they still find changes too late. Five to Nine watches competitor ads and landing pages on Meta, confirms the real changes, and sends proof-backed alerts by email so teams can act before the next workday starts.`

## Positioning Rules

- Lead with `proof-backed competitor monitoring`.
- Lead with `what changed` before `analysis`.
- Lead with trusted alerts before dashboards.
- Treat search as the hook, not the whole product.
- Keep `email` as the day-one delivery language. WhatsApp and Slack are dormant, non-GA channels — barred from customer-facing delivery claims until a provider is configured and Nish un-gates them.

## Do Not Lead With

- `competitor analysis workspace`
- `ad research product with alerts`
- `generic AI agent for marketers`
- `search layer over ad history`

## Homepage Direction

- Headline: `See what changed before your next standup.`
- Subhead: `Proof-backed competitor monitoring for growth teams.`
- Supporting idea: `The product is not manual research. The product is finding out in time, with proof.`

## Internal Rule

- When copy has to choose between the old research framing and the new monitoring framing, choose the monitoring framing.
