# Compare-page pricing sources

Every competitor price, brand count, or cadence quoted on a Five to Nine
marketing page must trace to a row in this file: the figure exactly as the
vendor's own page prints it (currency and billing period included), the URL,
and the date it was checked. Vendor pages change — figures are scoped to the
check date and are never restated as standing facts.
