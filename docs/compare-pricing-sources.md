# Competitor pricing sources (issue #2139)

Source record for the "Price of knowing" table on /pricing. Every figure below
is printed on the cited page; tracked-brand counts and cadences are recorded
only where the page prints them for that tier. Prices change — re-check the
page before editing the table, and update the `checked` date when you do.

## Foreplay Basic

- Entry price: $59 /month (monthly billing). Also printed: $49 /month when
  billed annually ("Save $120 annually").
- Tracked-brand count: not printed for Basic (the page prints "15 Brands" for
  the Workflow tier only).
- Cadence: not printed for Basic.
- Source: https://foreplay.co/pricing
- checked 2026-09-09

## Visualping Personal 1K

- Entry price: $14/mo (monthly billing). Also printed: $120/yr ($10/mo) on
  annual billing, and a free plan at $0/mo (5 pages, 150 checks a month).
- Tracked-brand count: 10 pages (1,000 checks a month).
- Cadence: Every 15 min.
- Source: https://visualping.io/pricing
- checked 2026-09-09

- Entry price: €99 /month (billed monthly). Also printed: $99/mo, and $990/yr

  activity plus a weekly email summary.
- Source: https://panoramata.co/pricing
- checked 2026-09-09
