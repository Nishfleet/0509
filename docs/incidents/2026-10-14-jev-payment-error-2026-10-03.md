# Jev refused with "2021: Payment error" for about two hours (2026-10-03)

## What customers saw

Mentions, site judgments and discovery that ran in that window were left unjudged until the next pass: 68 mentions at 01:00, 15 site judgments at 02:00 and more than 117 discovery calls at 03:00.

## Start and end (UTC)

2026-10-03 01:00 to about 03:00. Calls succeeded again from 03:00 even though the day's total was already above the 10,000 free neurons.

## Root cause

Not yet proven. Known: it is not the free-tier ceiling (calls kept working above it), the account was on a paid plan (1.1 million neurons used on 2026-10-02 without a failure), and it cleared on its own. That points to a transient billing error on Cloudflare's side. It was the day after the 1.1 million neuron day (see `2026-10-14-neuron-burn-2026-10-02.md`), which may be related.

## How it was detected

Sentry showed `site.jev_unavailable` 15 times on 2026-10-14's read.

## The fix

None needed in code: unjudged items are re-judged by the existing rejudge pass.

## What stops a repeat

1. Already in place: the rejudge pass and the `jev_unavailable` events.
2. Open: if this returns, the usage notification described in the neuron note would show whether spend is involved.
