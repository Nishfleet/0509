- Full description (use the paragraphs below; each claim is live-verifiable):
  Five to Nine monitors competitors' Meta ads and visible landing-page
  changes and turns them into source-backed evidence. It checks what
  changed — ads, offers, CTAs, and forms — and saves source-linked proof for
  each confirmed change, so a pricing change or a new offer arrives with
  screenshots instead of a rumor.

  Data is public-source only: the public Meta Ad Library — the same public
  archive anyone can open in a browser — plus the public landing pages those
  ads link to. Five to Nine never logs in to anything and never reads
  anything behind a login.

  Cadence limits: paid plans run scheduled checks every 3–6 hours (Scout
  every 6 hours, Starter every 3 hours, Agency every 3 hours for its first
  25 watchlists with the rest every 6 hours). Starter and Agency can turn on
  instant alerts, so a confirmed change emails you as soon as a check finds
  it. The free plan watches one competitor with a weekly scheduled check and
  a weekly email brief.
