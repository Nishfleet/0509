# Decision Context

## Decision

Can Five to Nine move from built product to launch-ready product without overstating what is live?

## Audience

Nish, pilot customers, and future operators reviewing the public product surface.

## What This Slice Proves

- The public site can honestly position the product as pilot-ready.
- Legal/trust basics exist in the active app.
- Agent-readable Markdown matches the visible product truth.
- Launch gates are explicit and runnable.

## What This Slice Does Not Prove

- Fresh commercial discovery is reliable.
- Self-serve billing works.
- Customer WhatsApp delivery is verified.
- Legal/compliance review is complete.
