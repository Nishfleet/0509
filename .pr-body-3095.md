## The diff

- `scripts/canary-timeline-coverage.mjs`: three-verdict contract — **pass** (exit 0, at/above floor above baseline), **pending** (exit 1, baseline held while the capture pipeline populates — the documented expected state), **regression** (exit 3, covered < 7: indexed proof pages vanished). `--file-issue` fires on regressions only — a pending night must never auto-file. Probe failure stays exit 2.
- `ops/timeline-coverage-guard/`: runner + systemd service + timer + provision script, sibling of `ops/demo-brand-timeline-guard/` (public sitemap probe — no Cloudflare token needed; ambient gh auth backs the regression auto-file, deduped). Daily 09:47 UTC, after the 04:00 capture rail. The unit SUCCEEDS on pending (a pinned-red timer adds noise, not signal), FAILS on regression/probe-failure, and fails loud on a stale checkout whose canary predates the verdict split (a silent downgrade of every alarm to observe must be impossible).
- `tests/timeline-coverage-canary.test.ts`: the verdict/exit contract locked (15 cases), including the exact regression/pending boundary at the seeded baseline.
