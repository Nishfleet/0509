| `/app/upgrade` | `app/routes/app.upgrade.ts` (an action, plus a loader that sends a plain GET to `/app/settings`), gate drawn by `app/components/plan-gate.tsx`, checkout in `app/lib/billing/checkout.server.ts` | visiting the URL by link redirects to Settings; the plan gate's **Upgrade to <plan>** button (role `button`) on `/app/competitors` posts here; `Tab` to it from the Add field, `Enter` | Creates a Dodo checkout session for the posted plan (`plan` is `scout`, `starter` or `agency`) through the official `dodopayments` SDK (`client.checkoutSessions.create`, product id from `DODO_PRODUCT_<PLAN>`, a 7-day trial, the workspace id, plan and a `proof` in metadata, return to `/app/competitors?upgraded=<plan>`) and answers 302 to Dodo's `checkout_url`. A customer who has paid before gets no second free trial: the checkout carries no trial and the gate says "You've already had your free trial, so you're billed today." If Dodo cannot say whether the old subscription was a paid one, no checkout starts and the customer gets the same "isn't available" line, so nobody is charged against terms they were shown. A subscription Dodo answers 404 for counts as a paid one. An unknown plan, a plan with no product id configured or a missing API key answers the one line "Upgrading isn't available right now. Try again in a few minutes." shown under the gate button. Signed in only; the workspace is the caller's own. A workspace whose plan is live (active, trialing or cancelled but paid through) is refused with "This workspace already has a plan. Change it from Settings." and no checkout is created. The action also reads an optional `interval` field (`monthly` by default, or `yearly`); a yearly checkout uses the plan's `DODO_PRODUCT_<PLAN>_YEARLY` product and is refused with the same "isn't available" message while that variable is empty, which it is until Nish approves yearly pricing.
