| weekly standing | `workers/standing/nightly.ts`, called from `workers/app.ts` `scheduled` on the `0 3 * * *` cron; the rollover is the `StandingRollover` Workflow (`workers/workflows/standing-rollover.ts`, binding `STANDING_ROLLOVER`, wrangler `workflows` block) | no route — the nightly cron, then one Workflow instance per workspace per week, id `rollover-<workspace>-<YYYYMMDDTHHMM>` | Engine 6 (0509#3978, 0509#4004, `docs/engines/standing-home.md`). Nightly, for every workspace: recompute this week's scores with rank and movement left empty, and make sure next brief's rollover instance exists (`createBatch` skips ids that already exist, so a second run creates nothing). The rollover sleeps until the workspace's brief time in its own timezone (`app/lib/brief-schedule.ts`), then in separate retried steps: final scores, freeze rank and movement (ties by score, then last week's rank), write the `digest` row as `pending` with a `BriefPayload`, put `{ digest_id }` on `send-email`, and create next week's instance. When the workspace has paused the brief (`workspace.brief_paused_at` set), the digest is written as `paused` and nothing goes on `send-email`; the sweeper only re-enqueues `pending`, so a paused brief is never sent. Home and history still read it. It stands down, writing nothing, when the workspace is gone or has moved its brief time, and writes no brief when fewer than two brands are ranked, because there is nothing to compare. A week with scores but no weekly `digest` row (held, or never run), more than an hour and at most 5 days past its close, gets a `-catch-up` instance from the next nightly run. Saving a different brief time whose slot started within the last hour includes that same `-catch-up` instance in the one `createBatch` the save already makes when the week that just came due was not already briefed. An unchanged time still creates nothing. Frozen weeks never change: the score upsert only touches rows with no rank. The brief's why-line is the quiet-week counts line until Jev's D4 `read_this_first` is wired. Proof: `tests/integration/standing/rollover.integration.test.ts` runs the real Workflow binding against local D1 — freeze, digest row and payload, successor, the schedule-moved stand-down, no brief with only the own brand on, and the nightly catch-up, plus the paused brief writing `paused` with no send and the resumed brief sending again; `tests/standing/brief-schedule.test.ts` covers the zone and daylight-saving math. Brand moves: `composeBrief` fills each brand line's `biggest_move` with the title (or summary, cut at 140 characters) of that brand's highest-weighted judged signal in the week, picked by the same `pickBiggestMove` the competitor page uses (`workers/standing/brand-moves.ts`); a brand with nothing scored keeps `null`, so a quiet week still names what each rival did. Proof: `tests/integration/brief.integration.test.ts`. **J11** — `e2e/j11-weekly-brief.spec.ts` reads the brief from the e2e inbox, checks the nothing-noteworthy order at 600 px with an off brand absent, and proves the one-click unsubscribe suppresses the next send. **J12** — `e2e/j12-rollovers.spec.ts` (production, the `j12` job in `e2e-scheduled.yml`, Wednesdays): the kept account `e2e+j12-rollovers@0509.io` watches four ON brands and turns `slack.com` off and on each run; the spec reads the two newest weekly briefs and Home, and checks that Home's headline, why-line and rows equal the newest brief, that the four-week table's stored ranks equal each of the two briefs, that every movement word is the change between the two stored weeks ("new" after a brand came back), and that a brand that went off is unranked with "<name> paused" in the why-line. Until two briefs exist the run only tends the account and reports skipped. A week with any change or mention still lacking a verdict is held (`held_unjudged`): no digest and no email until it is judged or 72 hours have passed (`app/lib/customer-ready.ts` `weekSendable`); the nightly catch-up uses a new instance id each night (`nightlyCatchUpInstance`) so a held week is retried. The digest stores the subject it sends. Before freezing, the rollover re-judges the week's unjudged mentions (`rejudge-mentions`, up to 60) and changes. |
