| own-site check | `workers/workflows/own-site-check.ts` (the `OwnSiteCheck` Workflow, binding `OWN_SITE_CHECK`, exported as `OwnSiteCheckWorkflow` from `workers/app.ts`) and `app/lib/site/own-site.server.ts` | no route — the `0 * * * *` Worker cron (`triggers.crons`, `startScheduledWorkflow` in `workers/workflow-crons.ts`) creates one instance every hour, on the hour, under the id `own-site-check-<instant>` | Engine 4's own-site lane. Gives every own brand with a registrable website a `home` page (independent of the `site.web` source switch), closes silently any open incident whose page is no longer an own homepage, then probes each own homepage once with a plain `fetch` (redirects followed, 10 s timeout, never the browser), one retried step per page. Broken means a 5xx, 404 or 410, or no answer from both the apex and `www.`; a 401, 403, 429 or a Cloudflare challenge (`cf-mitigated: challenge`) is unknown and never opens anything. A broken page is probed again five minutes later in its own step; only a second broken answer, in a separate `open` step, opens an `incident` (kind `error <status>` or `not loading`; the partial unique index allows one open incident per page), pins an `alert` (`kind = 'own_site_broken'`, severity `high`, title `<page host> looks broken: <kind>`) and puts `{ incident_id }` on `send-email`, which sends the incident email. Each later hour is the re-check that email promises: an open incident whose page reads clean is closed and its `{ incident_id }` goes on `send-email` again for the one "looks fixed" email. A step that still fails after its retries is logged and counted, and the run carries on with the next page. Competitor sites never open incidents. Soft breaks (a 200 page missing its pricing) wait for Jev D3s. Proof: `tests/integration/site/own-site.integration.test.ts` runs the real Workflow binding: healthy, broken then fixed, and competitor-never. |
