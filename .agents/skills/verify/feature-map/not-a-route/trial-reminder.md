| trial reminder | `workers/delivery/trial-reminder.ts`, template `workers/delivery/trial-template.ts`, reader `app/lib/data/trial_reminder.server.ts`, called from `workers/app.ts` `scheduled` on the `0 3 * * *` cron | no route — the nightly cron | Emails the workspace's verified delivery address once per trial, when the trial ends within two days: "Your <plan> trial ends on <weekday date>", what it costs a month after that, and that cancelling before then means no charge, with a link to Settings (**Manage or cancel plan**). Only a plan that is `active` with `trialing = 1` and a period end in the next two days qualifies, so a customer who cancelled, already paid or whose trial has ended gets nothing; a Tester subscription, a subscription Dodo cannot confirm and a bounced address are skipped and tried again the next night. The send goes through `sendMessage` like every other message; `send_attempt` holds `trial-ending:<workspace>:<period end>` as the sent-marker, so the reminder goes once and a failed send is retried.
