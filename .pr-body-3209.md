## Summary

- New `review_sites` presence connector (`app/lib/presence-connectors/review-sites.server.ts`): captures mentions for a tracked brand or person from review-site public pages into the shared mention table. First wired provider: Trustpilot business-unit review pages (`trustpilot.com/review/<domain>` — the page a human reads); the connector's contract is the schema.org/Review JSON-LD the page itself publishes. No account, no API key, no vendor.
- Dedup by canonical URL: each mention's canonical URL is the published `/reviews/<uuid>` permalink (Trustpilot's own identifier, path 301-confirmed 2026-09-14); re-polls of the ~20-review published window dedupe through the `(source_target_id, url_hash)` UNIQUE constraint in `upsertPresenceItems`.
- Per-source kill flag: `PRESENCE_REVIEW_SITES_ROLLOUT` (disabled default) through `evaluateConnectorAccessGate`; flag off → `connector_not_operational` with zero requests.
- Capture-validity gate: challenge pages (AWS-WAF / DataDome — both measured, challenge fixture committed) and unparsable bodies record honest failures (`review_site_challenge`, `review_site_parse_failed`, `rate_limited`); a business unit publishing its aggregateRating node but zero reviews is an honest empty. Nothing is ever fabricated.
- Coverage note states the public surface: `presenceSourceCoverageForDocs` /status row (gated) + `docs/mentions/PLAN.md` survey row say exactly what is covered (Trustpilot's published ~20-newest-reviews window, star ratings in raw_json) and what is not (G2/Capterra public pages are bot-verified — G2 DataDome, Capterra Cloudflare — and their documented APIs are partner/paid; those targets answer `provider_not_wired_yet`).
